Genomic Tokenization in Blockchain Using a Hybrid AES/ECC Encryption Scheme




The rapid exponential growth in genomic data presents great potential for biomedical applications as well as significant challenges in data security, privacy, and management. We propose a novel framework that integrates blockchain technology, InterPlanetary File System (IPFS), and advanced cryptography algorithms to address these challenges for secure and efficient genomic data management. The proposed framework leverages the blockchain's decentralized and immutable nature to ensure data integrity, transparency, and auditability while employing IPFS for scalable and cost-effective off-chain genomic data storage. A hybrid encryption scheme combining Advanced Encryption Standard (AES) and Elliptic Curve Cryptography (ECC) is implemented to provide strong security and privacy for genomic data. The framework incorporates smart contract-based access control mechanisms and auditing capabilities to enforce fine-grained access policies and maintain a tamper-proof data access and usage record. Experimental results demonstrate the superior performance of the proposed framework in terms of encryption time, decryption time, and data processing throughput compared to other state-of-the-art solutions. The proposed framework offers a comprehensive solution for genomic data integrity, security, privacy, and scalability, potentially accelerating the advancement of personalized medicine while ensuring high standards of data protection and patient privacy.
